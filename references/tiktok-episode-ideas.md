Keep the upper panel readable on a phone: crop to one important detail at a time, enlarge short text, and use a highlight or purposeful screen change as the explanation develops. # One CTA for every episode

**Join the Imladri waitlist at imladri.com and add your city.**

Speak that exact line at the end of every episode. Show the same message on the final upper-panel card. Do not add a second request to follow, comment, vote, or take a quiz.

# Parallel production

These numbers identify episodes; they are not a posting sequence. Keep all ten series active in parallel and mix topics across the daily schedule. For a seven-post day, a practical rotation is two dating-situation videos, two product or Your Type demonstrations, one pricing or matchmaking-services breakdown, one trust or feedback video, and one founder or city-launch update. Rotate the specific series and subjects across days.

Batch the upper-half assets first, then record the matching takes. Each episode follows one clear progression: hook, specific example, explanation, Imladri's actual or proposed response, and the fixed waitlist CTA. Reuse the editing template while changing the argument and the asset.

# Using these ideas

The hook is the opening line. The talking point is the single idea to explain. The upper-half direction tells the editor what to display. These are creative briefs, not evidence that every proposed feature already exists.

Use actual footage for available features. Keep a visible Demo or Concept label on mockups and fictional chats, and use sample or consenting adult profile images. For a competitor breakdown, capture the specific current public offer or policy, keep its source and capture date, and discuss only what the evidence supports. Use actual aggregate counts only when showing waitlist or city data.
