`service.py` explicitly makes no LLM calls. `safe_profile` reports `identity_verified: false`. `bootstrap` still contains a disabled $19/month future beta test and free-beta text. `launch` defaults to Chicago and has profile/discovery switches. All four are source facts to adapt, not new product decisions.

Two additional extraction gaps are mandatory work: `router.py` has a legacy member-photo read shortcut for `admin`/`staff`/`super_admin`, separate from its injected admin dependency; remove or replace it with the verified owner/member boundary. Profile review enforces `expected_revision`, while photo and report/appeal reviews need equivalent guards added. Preserve the original source as reference and prove the adapted behavior with the acceptance tests.

## Assessment content and historical account infrastructure

- [quiz.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/quiz.py) and [intimacy_catalog.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/intimacy_catalog.py) provide the original wording selected by `dating/catalog.py`. Reuse approved wording/IDs, never old user answers by implicit migration.
- [YourTypePage.jsx](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/frontend/src/pages/app/YourTypePage.jsx), [YourType.jsx](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/frontend/src/components/app/YourType.jsx), and [your_type.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/your_type.py) are deeper assessment references, not mandatory new-app screens or proof of a validated predictor.
- [server.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/server.py) shows host wiring: outer `/api`, injected `current_member` and `admin_dep`, database adapters, and legacy routes. Extract a narrow dating host rather than copying every coaching/marketplace service into production.
- [accounts.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/accounts.py), [clerk_auth.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/clerk_auth.py), and [postgres_migration.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/postgres_migration.py) are account lifecycle/auth/storage integration references. Verify current provider configuration and environment isolation. Do not deploy an old role, host allowlist or billing policy unexamined.

Regression references: [test_dating.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/tests/test_dating.py), [test_dating_host.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/tests/test_dating_host.py), [test_dating_review.py](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/backend/tests/test_dating_review.py), and [DatingPrivacy.test.jsx](https://github.com/try-imladri/imladri-dating-source/blob/5b9100518d3942c969bc87f8ad46a924e92d7312/frontend/src/components/dating/DatingPrivacy.test.jsx).
